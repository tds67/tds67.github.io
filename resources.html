KRG — Digital Transformation / e-Government information

KRG — Advanced Data Center / Government cloud initiatives

Infrastructure modernization is a major driver of cybersecurity needs: identity, access control, monitoring, incident response, and resilience become critical when services centralize.

International standards and frameworks

NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
Framework

A widely used framework to organize cyber risk management using functions like Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.

Open
ISO/IEC 27001 (Information Security Management)
Standard

The global standard for building an Information Security Management System (ISMS). Useful for government agencies, banks, telecoms, universities, and major enterprises.

Open
CIS Critical Security Controls
Best practices

Practical control set for reducing common attacks: asset inventory, secure configuration, vulnerability management, logging, backups, and more.

Open
ENISA — Guidance, reports, and good practice
Research / Guidance

Strong public resources on incident response, resilience, threats, and cyber governance.

Open

Threat landscape and targeting research

Recorded Future — KRG targeting research (examples)
Threat intelligence

Threat research can help explain why the region is targeted and what tactics are common. Use these for contextual risk discussion (phishing, credential theft, malware, espionage).

Open
MITRE ATT&CK (Tactics & Techniques)
Knowledge base

A globally used, public knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques. Useful for mapping likely attacks to real defensive controls and training scenarios.

Open

Capacity building and incident readiness

FIRST — Incident Response (CSIRT) community
Community / Practice

Guidance and community standards for incident response teams. Helpful if the region strengthens SOC/CSIRT cooperation across sectors.

Open
CISA — Cyber guidance and alerts
Public guidance

Practical advisories, known exploited vulnerabilities, and defensive guidance that can be adapted into local policies and patching programs.

Open
OWASP Top 10 (Web application security)
Secure development

A standard reference for common web security risks (injection, broken access control, etc.). Useful for universities, developers, government portals, and local software companies.

Open
